                          Maslow's Hierarchy of Jobs
                         +--------------------------+
                         |       INTERESTING        |
                         | Is the work interesting? |
                      +--------------------------------+
                      |            USEFUL              |
                      |       Is the work useful?      |
                   +--------------------------------------+
                   |             CONNECTED                |
                   |    Is the pay related to the work?   |
                +--------------------------------------------+
                |                STABILITY                   |
                |      Will the pay (and work) continue?     |
             +--------------------------------------------------+
             |                      PAY                         |
             |                 Will I get paid?                 |
          +--------------------------------------------------------+
          |                       ABILITY                          |
          |                  Can I do the work?                    |
       +--------------------------------------------------------------+
       |                           WORK                               |
       |                       Is there work?                         |
    +--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    |                             DEATH                                  |
    |                        Will it kill me?                            |
 +--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
 |                                MORAL                                     |
 |                      Is the base activity moral?                         |
